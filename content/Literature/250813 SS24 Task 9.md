### a) How to obtain the ML estimate for a multivariate Gaussian:

The maximum likelihood estimate finds parameters $\theta$ that maximize the likelihood of observing the training data:
$$
\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}^{ML} = \arg\max_{\boldsymbol{\theta}} L(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = \arg\max_{\boldsymbol{\theta}} \prod p(\mathbf{O}|\boldsymbol{\theta})
$$
In practice, we maximize the **log-likelihood**:
$$
\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}^{(\mathrm{ML})}=\arg \max _{\boldsymbol{\theta}} \mathrm{LL}(\boldsymbol{\theta})
$$
With log-likelihood $\mathrm{LL}(\boldsymbol{\theta})=\sum_{\tau=1}^T \log \mathrm{p}\left(\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{o}_\tau \mid \boldsymbol{\theta}\right)$

To find the parameters that maximize the likelihood of observing the training data, one must find the maximum of the log-likelihood function, $LL(\theta)$. This is typically done by taking the partial derivative with respect to each parameter, setting it to zero, and solving the resulting system of equations.

* **Mean**: The sample mean of the training data $O$.

	$$
	\hat{\mu} = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{\tau=1}^{T} o_{\tau}
	$$

* **Covariance**: The unbiased sample covariance of the training data $O$.

	$$
	\hat{\Sigma} = \frac{1}{T-1} \sum_{\tau=1}^{T} (o_{\tau} - \hat{\mu})(o_{\tau} - \hat{\mu})^{T}
	$$

### b) How to estimate parameters for a GMM:

The parameters of a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) are estimated using the **Expectation-Maximization (EM) algorithm**. This is an iterative algorithm that alternates between two steps:
1.  **E-step**: Calculates the posterior probabilities (responsibilities) of each data point belonging to each Gaussian component.
2.  **M-step**: Uses these responsibilities to re-calculate the GMM parameters (weights, means, and covariances).

### c) Number of learnable parameters for a GMM:

Given a data dimension $d=10$ and $K=8$ Gaussians:

* **Weights**: $K-1 = 8-1 = 7$
* **Means**: $K \times d = 8 \times 10 = 80$
* **Covariances (Full)**: $K \times \frac{d(d+1)}{2} = 8 \times \frac{10(11)}{2} = 440$
* **Covariances (Diagonal)**: $K \times d = 8 \times 10 = 80$

**Total Parameters:**
* **Full Covariance**: $7 + 80 + 440 = \textbf{527}$
* **Diagonal Covariance**: $7 + 80 + 80 = \textbf{167}$
