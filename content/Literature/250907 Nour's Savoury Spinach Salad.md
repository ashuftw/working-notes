## Ingredients
- Spinach 
- Onions 
- Chilly Paste 
- Lemon 
- Salt 
- Olive oil 
## Preparation 
- Cut and salt the spinach. 
- Let it rest and squeeze excess water. 
- Diced onions + Chilly paste 
- Season with olive oil and lemon juice
- Mix everything 
- Garnish with sliced lemon
- Mix and serve!
