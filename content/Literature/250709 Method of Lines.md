## Definition
It is a technique used to solve [[221110 Partial Differential Equations|Partial Differential Equations]]. The main idea is to transform a single [[221110 Partial Differential Equations|PDE]] which includes derivatives of both time and space into a system of [[221106 Ordinary Differential Equations|ODEs]], which only have derivatives of time. We do this by expressing each spatial domain (line) as a derivative of time.
