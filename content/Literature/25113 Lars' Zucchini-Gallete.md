## Ingredients 
- Zucchini 
- Blätterteig (Puff Pastry)
- Vegan Hirtenkäse alternative
- Vegan cream cheese alternative
- Garlic powder 
- Black pepper 
## Preparation
- Preheat oven to 200°C 
- Thinly slice 2 Zucchinis and season with salt to extract water. 
- Dab with cloth to remove released water 
- Spread cream cheese on the dough and season with garlic powder. 
- Arrange zucchini in overlapping pattern. 
- Sprinkle crushed Hirtenkäse and top with some black pepper powder. 
- Bake for about 20-30 minutes.
