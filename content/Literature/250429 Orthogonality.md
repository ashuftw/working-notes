## Meaning

In mathematics, orthogonality represents a general description of objects being *perpendicular* some sense. In the case of vectors, it is quite intuitive to check as the dot products of two pependicular (orthogonal) vectors is zero 
- Two vectors $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ are orthogonal if

$$
(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}):=\mathbf{a}^{\top} \mathbf{b}=0 .
$$

- Two functions $f, g$ are orthogonal if

$$
(f, g):=\int_a^b f(x) g(x) d x=0,
$$

the $\sin$, $\cos$ functions for instance.
- Two (mean-free) random variables $X, Y$ are orthogonal if they are uncorrelated, i.e.,

$$
(X, Y):=\mathbb{E}[X Y]=0 .
$$
