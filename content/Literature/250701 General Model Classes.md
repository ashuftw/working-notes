## **Linear Models:**
- **Polynomials** (y = w₀ + w₁x + w₂x² + ...)
- **Linear regression** (least squares)
## **Non-Linear Models:**
- **Neural Networks (NN)** - with hidden layers and non-linear activation functions
- **Radial Basis Function Networks (RBF)** - using Gaussian basis functions
## **Local Models:**
- **RBF** (Radial Basis Function networks)
- **LWR** (Locally Weighted Regression)
- **WR** (Weighted Regression)
- **Unified Model** (framework connecting the above)
## **Prototype-Based Models:**
- **k-means clustering**
- **GMM** (Gaussian Mixture Models)
- **VQ** (Vector Quantization)
## **Concept Learning Models:**
- **Decision Trees** (ID3 algorithm)
- **Naive Bayes** classifiers
- **Conjunctions of conditions** (attribute-based)
## **Lazy Learning Models:**
- **k-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)**
- **Nadaraya-Watson regressor**
