**Common Examples of Basis Functions:**

- **Polynomial:** $\phi_j(x) = x^j \rightarrow \phi(x) = [1, x, x^2, x^3, \ldots]^T$
- **RBF (Gaussian):** $\phi_j(x) = \exp\left(-\frac{|x - \mu_j|^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)$
- **Simple linear:** $\phi_j(x) = x_j$ (j-th component of input)
