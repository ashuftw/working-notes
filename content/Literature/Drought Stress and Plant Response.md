Question: Why isn't the Hydraulic Safety maximised? 
- larger xylem -> higher velocity -> more embolism
- plant optimizes between efficiency and safety

![[../../Files/Pasted image 20260616124016.png]]
isohydric strategy
what are the advantages 
- close stomata -> water potential in the xylem cannot drop more (strategy to reduce risk of embolism)
tradeoff 
- photosynthesis - no photosynthesis can occur when stomata is closed 

anisohydric strategy
what are the advantages 
- photosyntheis is longer -> more co2 capture
- water potential is more negative - more embolism 

iso and aniso is a spectrum. not a binary or a category


hydraulic failure -> damages organs of the plant 
carbon starvation -> 
biotic attack -> targets already weak plants and kills them 

-> the above factors can all happen leading to morality
