The **Hamming distance** is defined as the **number of non-matching bits** in two words (or binary indices) of the same length. In the context of a hypercube network, the shortest path length between any two nodes is equal to their Hamming distance. This distance is found by comparing their binary indices bit by bit and counting how many positions have different values. [[250801 Network Calculations|Example]]
