### Mathematical Properties
**For continuous random variables:**
- $F_X(x)$ is monotonically non-decreasing
- $\lim_{x \to -\infty} F_X(x) = 0$
- $\lim_{x \to \infty} F_X(x) = 1$
- Right-continuous: $F_X(x^+) = F_X(x)$
