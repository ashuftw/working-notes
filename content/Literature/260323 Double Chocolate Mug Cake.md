## Ingredients
**Dry**
- 15g flour (2 tbsp)
- 11g cocoa (2 tbsp)
- 25g sugar (2 tbsp)
- 1g baking powder (1/4 tsp)
- <1g salt (pinch)

**Wet**

- 45gm plant-based milk (3 tbsp)
- 14gm vegetable oil (1 tbsp)
- 1gm vanilla extract (1/4 tsp)
## Preparation 
- Combine the dry ingredients 
- Add wet ingredients and give it a coarse mix
- Microwave at 800 Watts for 2 Minutes
