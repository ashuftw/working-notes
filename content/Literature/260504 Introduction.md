## IPAT Identity
$$
\text{Impact}_\text{(Env)}= \text{Population}\cdot \text{Affluence}\cdot \text{Technology}
$$
$$
\text{Impact} = \text{Population}\cdot\frac{\text{Consumption}}{\text{Population}}\cdot\frac{\text{Impact}}{\text{Consumption}}
$$
- **Population**: continues to grow at an exponential rate. It cannot be used by engineers to bring out environmental impact. 
- **Affluence**: as the material standard of living improves, consumption increases alongside the population. 
- **Technology:** as technology improves, the environmental impact per unit consumption decreases i.e, the consumption gets a cleaner footprint. This is the only factor that is actionable by engineers.
