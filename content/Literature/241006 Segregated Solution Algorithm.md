A [[241006 Segregated Solution Algorithm|Segregated Solution Algorithm]] solve the Governing Equations separately and in sequence. This convergence of such a solver is slower than a [[cfd coupled solver|coupled solver]]. However, it is less RAM intensive. [^1]
