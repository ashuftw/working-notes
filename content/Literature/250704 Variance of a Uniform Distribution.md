The general notation is:
$$
X \sim \mathcal{U}(a, b)
$$
- $a$ is the minimum value (lower bound).
- $b$ is the maximum value (upper bound).

The variance is calculated using the formula:
$$
\mathbb{V}[X]=\frac{(b-a)^2}{12}
$$

A special case is a uniform distribution defined symmetrically around a mean $\mu$, such as $X \sim \mathcal{U}(\mu-\Delta, \mu+\Delta)$.

In this case, the formula simplifies to:
$$
\mathbb{V}[X]=\frac{\Delta^2}{3}
$$
