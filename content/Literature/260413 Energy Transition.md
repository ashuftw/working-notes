## Urgency: Planetary Boundaries 
Planetary boundaries define a safe operating space for life on earth, based on the earth's ability to absorb human impact. Breaking the boundary results in changing those operating spaces irreversibely. 
![[../../Files/Pasted image 20260413165446.png|center|450]]

- 7 of the 9 planetary boundaries have been crossed. 
- fossil fuels contribute to 75% of all global emitted greenhouse gases. 
## Energy Carbon Nexus 
![[../../Files/Pasted image 20260413170425.png|center|450]]
It a triangular dependency trying to reduce one of the corners results in increase of demand for the other. Example: 
- depletion of materials means that more energy has to be used for extraction. 
- reducing the carbon intensity means that more materials are needed for the infrastructure needed to produce the energy (wind farm, solar cells etc)
