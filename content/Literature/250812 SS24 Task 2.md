#### b) Diagram with Overcrowding and Probability Formula

To calculate the probability of overcrowding, $P(o)$, we need to marginalize the joint probability distribution over the other variables $(P, C, H)$. The joint probability based on the diagram is:

$$
P(p, c, h, o) = P(p) \cdot P(c|p) \cdot P(h|p) \cdot P(o|h)
$$

The formula to calculate $P(o)$ by summing over all possible states of $P, C,$ and $H$ is:

$$
P(o) = \sum_{p} \sum_{c} \sum_{h} P(p, c, h, o)
$$


$$
P(o) = \sum_{p} \sum_{c} \sum_{h} P(p) \cdot P(c|p) \cdot P(h|p) \cdot P(o|h)
$$
