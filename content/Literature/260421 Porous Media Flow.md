Preferential flow: 
- it's when water flows through micro pores created by earthworms, roots etc. (animal activity)
- if we aggregate all the flow effects then the bulk of flow can be explained by darcy's law 
	![[../../Files/Pasted image 20260421114916.png]]
	$$
\begin{aligned}
&v_f=-K \frac{\Delta \psi}{\Delta \ell} \approx-K \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial \ell}\\
&\text { K - hydraulic conductivity (m/sPa) }
\end{aligned}
$$
- The Darcy velocity gives the average velocity of the fluid with which it crosses a specific length of the porous media. 
	![[../../Files/Pasted image 20260421115527.png]]
- We use the lumped way (darcy) in both plants AND soild because the specifications of the Xylem geometry  and the micropores in the soil cannot be knows.
